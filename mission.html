Our Mission

Project Balancē works to make dance and creative expression more accessible, welcoming, and meaningful for local communities.

What?

Project Balancē provides dancers with a safe and supportive environment, offering resources, emotional and physical support, and partnerships with local dance communities. In the spring of 2027 we will be hosting an event for dancers of all ages. There will be crafts and games for our younger participants, and workshops for dancers of all skill levels. We also plan to have both a dietitian and a psychologist present to give a talks on nutrition and mental health, respectively, and to provide support for our dancers.

Why?

To the outside world, dance is minimized as a simple 'hobby.' But anyone in the studio knows that dance means 13+ hours a week of dedication, because however bruised, however sprained, however bleeding, however aching, we keep going. Teachers still push our technique, but the industry leaves us completely unequipped to handle the rest. We are given no practical resources to navigate grueling schedules, identity blocks, or structural injuries. Dance isn't a hobby. Dance means passion, dedication, pain, and love all at once. Dancers dance because they need an escape, a passion, a safe place. A safe place that provides comfort and joy yet shatters their heart every time someone assumes that dance doesn’t hurt. Every time you are left with self-doubt, body anxiety, or an injury you don't know how to fix, you are left holding missing pieces. The industry leaves us with so many questions—and no answers. I built Project Balancē to be that answer. A home. A community that provides dancers a safe place. We offer resources with the answers to your in-the-wings thoughts, direct emotional and physical support, and, most importantly, partnerships with under-resourced local Texas dance communities.
