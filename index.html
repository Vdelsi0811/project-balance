ABOUT US

What is Project Balancē?

Project Balancē is a student led initiative focused on creating meaningful change in the dance community of Houston, Texas and hopefully further our reach. It was created as a way to give back with meaningful projects that support people within our community. We will focus on one larger-scale project in the spring as well as smaller seasonal initiatives. Throughout the year, our seasonal projects allow us to focus on specific needs, causes, and opportunities to help in ways that are manageable but still meaningful. These smaller projects give us the opportunity to stay involved, connect with different parts of our community, and continue making an impact throughout the year. Each spring, we plan to bring everything we have learned and built throughout the year into our largest project, which will involve greater fundraising, community participation, and outreach. As a first-year organization, Project Balancē is still at the beginning of its story, and we see that as an opportunity. We want Project Balancē to be an organization that grows with the people it serves and the people who choose to support it. We believe that service should feel personal, intentional, and connected to the real needs of a community. Through creativity, collaboration, fundraising, volunteering, and community involvement, we hope to create projects that people are genuinely excited to participate in.
